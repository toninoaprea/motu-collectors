1 · GITHUB CONNECTION

Photos are published straight to your GitHub repository. Paste a fine-grained token (only toninoaprea/motu-collectors, permission Contents: Read and write). It stays only in this browser.

2 · CHOOSE THE CHARACTER

3 · PHOTOS

Order: blister, card back, front, back, then extras. Empty slots keep the current photo (or stay empty). Click a slot or drop a file on it.

4 · MUSEUM SHELF FIGURE

PNG of the front photo without background (e.g. from remove.bg). Optional: if you skip it, the current one stays.

5 · CHECKLIST HEAD

Choose which picture to take the head from (the Shelf PNG is cleanest, but any photo works), then click on the face to place the square; use the slider for the size.

6 · PRICES (USD)

Average prices for items in good condition, in USD. Type them in the table, or export a CSV, fill it in Excel / Sheets and import it back. Empty cells keep the current value; the retail price is used until a price is set. Nothing goes online until you press PUBLISH PRICES.
