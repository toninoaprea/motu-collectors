THE HISTORY

MASTERS OF THE UNIVERSE

From a Mattel toy line to a global franchise spanning four decades — the story of He-Man, Skeletor, and the world of Eternia.

BY THE POWER OF GRAYSKULL

In 1982, Mattel launched one of the most iconic action figure lines in history. Masters of the Universe introduced the world to He-Man — the most powerful man in the universe — and his arch-nemesis Skeletor. What started as a toy line became a global cultural phenomenon, spanning animated series, comics, films, and decades of collector passion.

ERA BY ERA

40+ Years of History

CLASSIC LINE
1982
The Original Toy Line

Mattel launches the Masters of the Universe line with the first wave featuring He-Man, Skeletor, Teela, Man-At-Arms, Beast Man, Mer-Man, Stratos, and Zodac — each 5.5 inches tall with a unique action feature. Battle Cat and Castle Grayskull complete the first year.

ANIMATED SERIES
1983
He-Man Comes to TV

Filmation's He-Man and the Masters of the Universe premieres — one of the first animated series ever created specifically to support a toy line. It runs for 130 episodes and becomes one of the most-watched cartoons of the decade.

SHE-RA
1985
Princess of Power

She-Ra — He-Man's twin sister — gets her own toy line and animated series. The Princess of Power line runs until 1987, expanding the MOTU universe with iconic characters like Adora, Glimmer, Bow, and the villain Hordak.

FILM
1987
The Live-Action Movie

Dolph Lundgren stars as He-Man and Frank Langella as Skeletor in the first live-action Masters of the Universe film. A beloved cult classic among collectors and fans worldwide.

CLASSIC LINE
1988
End of the Classic Era

The original toy line concludes after seven waves. Over 70 action figures plus creatures, vehicles, and playsets — many with international variants across Europe, Latin America, and Asia — making the complete collection one of the most challenging in toy history.

NEW ADVENTURES
1989
He-Man in Space

He-Man returns with a futuristic sci-fi makeover in The New Adventures of He-Man. The line runs until 1992 with a fresh animated series, taking the story from Eternia to outer space.

200X
2002
The Modern Reboot

Mattel revives MOTU with a new animated series and redesigned toy line featuring detailed sculpts and modern articulation. The 200X line is highly regarded by collectors for its quality and faithful reimagining of the classic characters.

CLASSICS
2008
Masters of the Universe Classics

Mattel launches the premium Classics line exclusively through MattyCollector.com. Monthly subscription drops, highly detailed figures, and an interconnected lore make this the definitive collector line — later continued by Super7.

ORIGINS
2020
Back to the Roots

Masters of the Universe Origins launches — faithfully recreating the look of the 1982 classics with updated articulation. Origins reignites collector passion and introduces MOTU to a new generation worldwide.

MASTERVERSE
2021
Premium Collector Line

Masterverse debuts as Mattel's premium 7-inch collector line, drawing from all eras of MOTU. With superior articulation and detailed sculpts, it becomes the go-to line for serious adult collectors.

FILM · 2026
2026
A New Era Begins

A brand-new live-action Masters of the Universe film marks the biggest moment for the franchise in decades, bringing a wave of new collector interest and renewed passion for everything MOTU — vintage and modern alike.
